## Lucid Mode (New Game+)
Unlocked after any ending. The player knows the twist and so does the Voice.
- Alternate Voice lines in every chapter (EGG_007–008 and more to write in v2).
- The Pull is visible earlier and can be approached; it doesn't catch, it just glows warmer.
- Leaked objects glow brighter; the leaks are clearer and less muffled.
