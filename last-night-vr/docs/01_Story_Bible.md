# LAST NIGHT — Story Bible

> A man dies in his sleep and walks through a dream built from his life.
> He thinks he is escaping. He is actually refusing to be saved.

## Logline

At 3:12 AM, Tomas Reyes's heart stops in his sleep. In the dream, he walks a vast,
foggy field of memories, guided by a gentle inner Voice and hunted by a tall
figure of light. The figure is his wife Maren, doing CPR on the bedroom floor.
The dream is the last place their daughter Wren is still alive, and Tomas does
not want to wake up.

## Themes

| Theme | How it shows up |
| --- | --- |
| Grief that freezes you | Tomas stopped living when Wren died. The dream is a frozen version of that. |
| Rescue that feels like attack | The Pull (the thing chasing you) is love, seen by a mind that wants to stay. |
| Memory decays | Every place loses detail the longer you stay. You can't hold on. |
| Choosing to live is the hard choice | The "good" ending means giving something up. |

## The timeline (real world)

| Time | Event |
| --- | --- |
| 1982 | Tomas born. |
| 1988 (age 6) | Nearly drowns at Lake Verda. His father Elio pulls him out and breathes for him. (The game's first CPR clue.) |
| 1996 (age 14) | Ms. Adair's class. He stutters through a speech: "When I grow up I want to build houses that keep people safe." |
| 2004 (age 22) | Meets Maren on the Line 4 platform. She drops her red scarf. He runs after the train with it. |
| 2009 | They marry. Small apartment, yellow kitchen. |
| 2013 | Wren is born. |
| 2020 | Wren, age 7, gets sick suddenly (a fast-moving infection). 9 days in hospital. Tomas steps out for coffee at 3:10 AM. She dies at 3:12 AM. He was not in the room. |
| 2020–2026 | Tomas goes quiet. Works, sleeps, barely talks. Maren, an ER nurse, keeps going and keeps reaching for him. |
| Tonight, 3:12 AM | Cardiac arrest in his sleep. Maren wakes, calls 911 on speaker, starts compressions on the floor. Paramedics arrive at about 3:21 AM. |

The real-world event lasts about 11 minutes. The dream lasts as long as the player plays.

## Characters

### Tomas Reyes (the player)
- 44, architectural draftsman. Draws buildings for a living (that's why the dream is full of architecture).
- Never seen in full. The player sees his hands: a draftsman's calloused right index finger and a wedding ring.
- Rarely speaks. When he does it's quiet and short. Most of his inner life is spoken by the Voice.

### The Voice (narrator)
- Tomas's own inner voice. Calm, warm, close to the ear, always half a step too sure.
- Its goal is to keep Tomas in the dream. It never lies outright; it leaves things out.
- Its arc: comforting → protective → desperate → silent → (Wake ending) it finally says goodbye.
- Signature line: "Just keep walking. I'll be right here."

### Maren Reyes
- 43, ER nurse. Practical, funny, stubborn. Loved Tomas through six silent years.
- In the dream she appears three ways:
  1. **Memory Maren**: the younger her in memories (station, kitchen). Warm, teasing.
  2. **Leaked Maren**: her real voice tonight, muffled and fragmented, counting compressions and saying his name.
  3. **The Pull**: how Tomas's mind shows her rescue — a tall white shape that moves on the rhythm of her hands.
- Her key real-world lines are counting ("twenty-eight, twenty-nine, thirty") and "Don't you leave me too."

### Wren Reyes
- Died at 7. Loved drawing, rubber ducks, the color yellow, and a song called "Little Lantern" Tomas made up.
- In the dream she exists only in the last room and in her drawings hidden throughout the world.
- She knows more than the Voice does. At the end she is the one who tells him to go.

### The Pull
- The antagonist, as far as the player knows. A tall, thin, glowing silhouette, like a person-shaped doorway of light.
- Moves only in steps, on the beat of the compressions (about 110 BPM). Freezes when looked at directly.
- Never attacks. If it reaches the player, the world whites out and the player is thrown back to the last anchor entrance ("you almost woke up").
- After the reveal, it can be walked into on purpose. That's the Wake ending.

## The twist, layer by layer

The game peels back four layers. Each one re-explains everything before it.

1. **"I'm stuck in a nightmare."** (Prologue–Ch.2) The Voice is a guide; the Pull is a monster.
2. **"I died in my sleep."** (Ch.3–Ch.4) The Voice admits it. "Let it finish. It doesn't hurt."
3. **"Something is trying to erase me."** (Ch.4–Act 3) The Pull gets closer. The Voice begs you to hide by closing your eyes.
4. **"Maren is trying to save me, and I don't want to go back."** (Ch.6) The reveal. The thumps were compressions. The flashes were the defibrillator. The beeping was the monitor. The "monster" is rescue. Hiding by closing your eyes was refusing to wake up.

A last sting: the Voice was never a guide. It is the part of Tomas that wanted to die six years ago and finally got its chance.

| **MAREN'S WORDS** (secret) | Collect all 5 leaked objects, then choose WAKE. | Extra epilogue in the hospital, 2 days later, from Maren's side of the bed. She tells him everything she said while he was gone. Final card: "Morning." |

## Tone rules for writers
- Quiet beats loud. No screaming monsters, no gore.
- Every scare should mean something on a second playthrough.
- Nobody says "you're dead" or "she's saving you" until Chapter 6.
- Wren is never a ghost or a jump scare. She is only ever warm.
